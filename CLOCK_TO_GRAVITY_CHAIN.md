# From the Q clock to the gravity response

The remaining bridge can now be written as one explicit premise followed by
an exact chain.

The premise is:

> The orientation-even gravitational prepotential channel realizes one
> quartic clock translation on the exponential horizon-area weight.

For the positive root `Q` of `Q^4 = Q + 1`, one clock translation is a shift
by `log Q`.  Acting on `exp(x)`, it has the exact eigenweight

\[
  \exp(x-\log Q)=\frac1Q\exp(x).
\]

The new Lean theorem expresses `1/Q` through the clock action rather than as
an unrelated constitutive coefficient.  A calibration theorem also proves
that, for this linear exponential eigenmode, the clock-action premise is
mathematically equivalent to assigning the even channel the weight `1/Q`.
This is a physically clearer formulation of the carrier premise, not a proof
of the carrier identification.  If the two
gravitational channels are also exchange symmetric and their diagonal mean is
normalized to one, elementary matrix uniqueness then forces

\[
  M_Q=
  \begin{pmatrix}
    1&-\lambda_4\\
    -\lambda_4&1
  \end{pmatrix},
  \qquad \lambda_4=1-\frac1Q.
\]

Its determinant is consequently

\[
  \det M_Q=1-\lambda_4^2=\frac{2Q-1}{Q^2}=S_Q.
\]

The same `lambda4` is proved independently to equal both the renewal-letter
frequency of the founding quartic substitution and the biorthogonally
normalized residual of its Perron transfer operator.  Thus the coefficient
in the gravity block is the coefficient already generated by the quartic
dynamics; it is not inserted after the fact.

\[
  S_Q E(h)=T,

physical response is inverse to the kinetic coefficient, the ordinary-source

screening formula.

## What has and has not been proved

The quartic clock eigenweight, Perron identities, matrix uniqueness,
determinant, and full sourced-operator reduction are exact kernel-checked
mathematics.

The one remaining physical identification is the premise quoted above: that
the gravitational dual-prepotential even channel is the carrier of the
quartic clock action.  The proof no longer assumes `lambda4`, the full response
matrix, or `S_Q` independently, but its calibration theorem shows that the
clock action and the even weight are equivalent descriptions of the same
physical input.  Establishing that carrier identification
from a microscopic PDT curvature action would convert this conditional chain
into a derivation of the screening mechanism.
