# From the cubic--quartic split to a continuous spacetime modular algebra

## The result in one sentence

The cubic and quartic substitutions supply two discrete modular clocks with
incommensurate frequencies.  Their synchronized step is the PDT scale
`rho*Q`, while their full joint modular spectrum is continuous in closure and
has the hyperfinite type `III_1` factor class expected for local relativistic
quantum field theory.

This gives `rho`, `Q`, and `rho*Q` different mathematical jobs.  It also
connects the common scale in the existing gravity formula to a standard
operator-algebraic construction.

## Two graph clocks

The cubic and quartic incidence matrices are

```text
        [[0,0,1],                    [[0,0,0,1],
A_rho =  [1,0,1],             A_Q =   [1,0,0,1],
         [0,1,0]]                      [0,1,0,0],
                                        [0,0,1,0]].
```

Lean proves:

| Graph | Primitive-power witness | Coprime return loops | Positive Perron value | Modular ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| cubic | every entry of `A_rho^5` is positive | `3, 5` | `rho` | `1/rho` |
| quartic | every entry of `A_Q^10` is positive | `4, 7` | `Q` | `1/Q` |

The standard finite-graph KMS theorem fixes the critical inverse temperature
of each canonical gauge system at the logarithm of its Perron value.  The
factor-classification theorem then gives hyperfinite type `III_(1/rho)` and
`III_(1/Q)` equilibrium factors.

## Why the two clocks cannot collapse to one

The independence is exact arithmetic.

Lean proves Selmer irreducibility for both defining polynomials, then proves

```text
[Q(rho):Q] = 3,
[Q(Q):Q]   = 4,
Q(rho) intersection Q(Q) = Q.
```

The last equality follows from the coprime field degrees.  Since `rho` is an
algebraic unit larger than one, no nonzero power of it can be rational.  Hence

```text
rho^a Q^b = 1,  a,b in Z
        implies
a = b = 0.
```

Lean then proves the logarithmic consequence

```text
log(rho) / log(Q) is irrational.
```

Thus there is no common period that turns both graph clocks into powers of one
smaller clock.  The additive group

```text
Z log(rho) + Z log(Q)
```

is dense in the real line, and the multiplicative group generated by
`1/rho` and `1/Q` is dense in the positive reals.

## Why type III_1 appears

Take the tensor product of the two graph-KMS equilibrium factors with their
product state.  Its modular action restricts to the original action on each
tensor factor.  The quartic factor is therefore a modularly covariant
subfactor of the combined system, and likewise for the cubic factor.

The joint modular ratios are

```text
rho^m Q^n,  m,n in Z.
```

Because the log ratio is irrational, those ratios do not form a discrete
cyclic set.  The Araki--Woods classification of hyperfinite infinite tensor
product factors, in the form stated explicitly by Margetts and Srinivasan,
puts a finite-rank system with two such logarithmically incommensurate
eigenvalues in type `III_1` rather than type `III_lambda`.

This is the same factor class that occurs generically for local algebras in
relativistic quantum field theory.  It is an abstract compatibility theorem:
it does not yet identify this particular product state with the vacuum state
of a gravitational horizon.

## Why the product rho*Q appears

For a complex modular parameter `z`, let

```text
F_rho(z) = exp(-i log(rho) z),
F_Q(z)   = exp(-i log(Q) z).
```

Lean proves their exact product law

```text
F_rho(z) F_Q(z)
  = exp(-i (log(rho)+log(Q)) z)
  = exp(-i log(rho Q) z)
  = F_(rho Q)(z).
```

On real modular time this joint flow has norm one.  On one synchronized
negative-imaginary step,

```text
F_(rho Q)(-i) = 1/(rho Q).
```

So the common PDT ruler is not inserted after the two systems are combined.
It is the inverse of their synchronized modular contraction.  Repeating or
counting the inverse step naturally produces integer powers of `rho*Q`.

The full joint spectrum and the synchronized diagonal step do different
things.  The irrational two-frequency spectrum supplies the continuous
type-`III_1` environment.  The diagonal step supplies the single shared
`rho*Q` scale used by the coupling grammar.

## Fixed conversion to geometric boost time

For any wedge algebra satisfying the Bisognano--Wichmann convention,

```text
Delta^(i t) = U(Lambda(-2 pi t)).
```

The conversion is fixed by relativistic modular theory. It is not a parameter
that can be adjusted to make the PDT scale agree with a desired frequency.
Lean now proves that the joint modular multiplier becomes exactly the boost
eigenphase with spectral weight

```text
omega_(rho Q) = log(rho Q)/(2 pi).
```

It also proves that this weight is positive for the positive cubic and quartic
roots. Thus the arithmetic fixes the frequency and the
Bisognano--Wichmann theorem fixes its conversion to rapidity.

This is a conditional spacetime statement. The Lean identity is exact under
the displayed convention; the remaining physical task is to prove that the
graph-product representation is the relevant gravitational wedge or
null-horizon algebra.

## Where the quartic screening factor belongs

The joint step does not replace the quartic defect.  The stages are

```text

                                      \

                                      /
quartic graph -> 1/Q -> lambda4 = 1-1/Q
                            -> S_Q = 1-lambda4^2.
```

The `rho*Q` factor is common to the full response.  The `Q` residue distinguishes
the gravitational two-channel correction.  In the existing coupling grammar,
Lean proves

```text

  = (rho Q)^224 S_Q.
```

The new combined capstone proves that identity alongside the synchronized
modular ratio `1/(rho Q)`.  The determinant exponent follows from the stated

## Time, settlement, and the Pisot divide

The real modular flow supplies the unit-circle motion of a clock.  The arrow
requires a separate irreversible feature.  On the cubic side, the non-Perron
conjugates have modulus `rho^(-1/2) < 1`, giving the proved decay rate

```text
-log |sigma_rho| = (1/2) log rho.
```

The separate chiral-Padovan formalization proves that the factor language has
an orientation-sensitive witness.  Together these mathematical facts give
the proposed time interpretation: contraction makes alternatives settle;
chirality selects an ordering of the history; the norm-one modular phase keeps
the clock moving.

The quartic root is non-Pisot, so its off-Perron data do not all contract.
The proposed PDT correspondence reads that arithmetic split physically:

| Arithmetic behavior | Proposed physical role |
|---|---|
| cubic/Pisot convergence | stable classical records and time's arrow |
| quartic/non-Pisot persistence | nonclassical information that does not globally settle |
| joint incommensurate modular spectrum | continuous local spacetime arena |
| synchronized `rho*Q` step | common interaction ruler |
| quartic residue and completion | gravitational screening channel |

The left column is mathematical.  The right column is the physical
identification to be tested.

## What remains physically

The operator-algebraic existence problem is now reduced.  The two graph
factors have an abstract hyperfinite type-`III_1` product representation in
which each factor is modularly covariant and the `rho*Q` diagonal step is
exact.

The remaining spacetime problem is to identify that representation with a
null-horizon observable algebra so that:

1. product modular time agrees with the geometric null or boost flow without
   rescaling;
2. the embedded spin-two modes preserve the null canonical potential and
   flux already formalized in this repository;
3. the physical visible-channel restriction yields the quartic `S_Q`
   response; and
4. the 224-direction determinant is the response counted by the gravitational
   coupling, rather than merely an isomorphic finite block.

Failure of any one of those requirements would break the proposed physical
identification while leaving the arithmetic and operator-algebra theorems
intact.
