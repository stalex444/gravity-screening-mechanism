# LQG, Jacobson, and the quartic horizon clock

## Verdict

The Barbero--Immirzi parameter should not carry the central PDT claim.  The
useful common structure is independent of any selected numerical value:

1. a local horizon has an area/flux observable;
2. its physical time evolution is generated by a Lorentz boost;
3. the exterior state is thermal in the KMS sense;
4. Jacobson converts an independently supplied entropy density into Newton's
   coupling.

LQG supplies concrete realizations of the first three items.  Jacobson supplies
the fourth implication.  PDT supplies a graph-selected modular step and a
two-channel response.  The exact remaining task is to intertwine the PDT graph
mode with the LQG horizon boost mode while placing its exterior/hidden split in
Jacobson's entropy and heat variables.

## Why the Immirzi value cancels

In Bianchi's quantum Rindler horizon, one facet with spin label `j` has

```text
area        A_f = 8 pi G hbar gamma j,
boost energy E_f = hbar gamma j a,
temperature T    = hbar a/(2 pi).
```

Therefore

```text
E_f/T = 2 pi gamma j = A_f/(4 G hbar).
```

The acceleration cancels, and the Immirzi factor is absorbed into the physical
area.  At fixed physical area the entropy is independent of the numerical
Immirzi label.  `GravityScreening/LQGHorizonThermodynamics.lean` proves these
equalities and the fixed-area independence statement in the kernel.

This is also why a numerical coincidence between a PDT constant and one
particular real Immirzi estimate cannot be the foundation of the gravity
argument.  Different horizon quantizations treat the parameter differently.
Bianchi obtains the macroscopic `A/(4 G hbar)` law for all real values in his
setup.  Pranzetti instead obtains a horizon KMS construction through analytic
continuation to the self-dual value.  Their robust overlap is boost
thermality and the area law, not a preferred real decimal.

## What LQG contributes

The isolated-horizon boundary condition relates connection curvature to the
triad flux.  Ashtekar, Baez, and Krasnov describe the curvature and triad as
fluctuating in tandem.  The Lean theorem
`scalarHorizonBoundary_transports_response` proves the finite local transport
algebra: if the same nonzero boundary coefficient relates the two sides before
and after a response, that coefficient cancels and the curvature response is
carried to the triad flux.

Pranzetti constructs a horizon-localized subalgebra of the LQG holonomy--flux
algebra and an automorphism generated by the boost operator.  The exterior

object for PDT's quartic graph-KMS sector.

Neither construction selects `log Q`.  LQG supplies the carrier and the boost
flow; the quartic graph supplies the arithmetic modular frequency.

## Why Jacobson is decisive

Jacobson assumes a local horizon entropy density `eta`,

```text
delta S = eta delta A,
```

and requires the Clausius relation for every local Rindler horizon.  The
Einstein equation follows with

```text
G = 1/(4 hbar eta).
```

Consequently a universal constant response

```text
eta_Q = S_Q eta_0
```

forces

```text
G_Q = G_0/S_Q.
```

Because `S_Q` is a constant, this remains an equilibrium rescaling.  The
non-equilibrium entropy-production terms studied by Eling, Guedens, and
Jacobson become relevant if the entropy response varies with curvature or
position.

Jacobson also exposes a cancellation test.  A common scaling of heat flux and
entropy does not alter Newton's coupling.  The PDT placement must therefore be
asymmetric:

```text
physical total boost heat:       scale 1,
accessible horizon entropy rate: scale S_Q,
Newton coupling:                 scale 1/S_Q.
```

The finite erasure dilation already proves the mathematical shape required by
this placement: its exterior observable scales by `S_Q`, its hidden complement
supplies `1-S_Q`, and their sum is conserved.  The LQG surface bridge proves
that the same determinant can scale the accessible area channel.  Calling the
conserved total observable Jacobson heat and the exterior area/information
channel Jacobson entropy is still a physical correspondence, not a consequence
of the finite linear algebra.

## The roles of rho and Q

The local horizon theorem isolates the quartic determinant because that is the
screening operation:

```text
det(M_Q) = S_Q = (2Q-1)/Q^2.
```

It is not the complete PDT gravitational scale.  The full displayed coupling
contains the joint cubic--quartic ruler and the gravitational exponent:

```text
alpha_G = pi^4 / ((rho Q)^224 S_Q).
```

The kernel theorem `rhoQClock_fullGravity_horizonEntropy_capstone` now displays
both pieces at once:

```text

  = det((rho Q) I_224) det(M_Q) / pi^4,
det(M_Q) = S_Q.
```

Thus `rho Q` controls the full bulk determinant, while `Q` controls the local
two-channel horizon response.  Conflating them would erase the architecture of
the theory.
